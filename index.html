Update 2

Planned reworks

  • Esoteric OrderPlanned rework

    A more active and varied playstyle, rather than relying on a Ritual for three Elder Signs. Remove the underused Moon Beast monsters, strengthen the faction’s identity as a powerful cult, and emphasize its ocean presence with Deep Ones as its core Monster.

  • WeaverPlanned rework

    Reduce unnecessary complexity and introduce standard Cultists. Rework Cosmic Web into an Area token that can have both positive and negative effects on other factions.

  • Blasphemy TwinsPlanned rework

    Redesign the concept around stronger synergy between the two Great Old Ones, and fix several problematic rules loops.
